MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted for RetroXR as atari_2600_console.glb:

  * Split the original scene into per-part GLBs. This file is the console only;
    the joystick, paddle, both plug ends and the cartridge ship separately.

  * Removed every Atari trademark. The source carries four, all as separate
    geometry: the front wordmark (a white decal plus its embossed relief), one
    on the joystick top face, and one on each plug end.

  * Removed the cartridge that was seated in the slot, along with its label art.
    The slot ships empty so the game can seat a cart of its own.

  * Split the six front-panel switches into individually named, individually
    pivoted objects (Switch_Power, Switch_TVType, Switch_LeftDifficulty,
    Switch_RightDifficulty, Switch_GameSelect, Switch_GameReset). Each keeps the
    dark filler mesh that sits behind its lever, so no hole is exposed as it
    travels.

  * Normalised switch rest positions. The source modelled Left Difficulty and
    Game Select thrown down and the other four up; all six now ship up, and the
    7.07 mm gap between those two groups is used as the switch throw.

  * Re-centred on the console's own footprint at real-world scale, resting on
    y = 0 rather than centred on its middle.

NOT modified: "video computer system" remains on the top control panel. Unlike
the four logos it is painted into the face-plate texture rather than being
separate geometry, and that same texture carries the functional switch legends.
