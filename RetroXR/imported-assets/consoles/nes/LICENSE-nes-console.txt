MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted for RetroXR as nes_console.glb and nes_plug.glb:

  * Removed the bundled controller (Case_25) and its cord mesh. RetroXR uses a
    separate controller model and drives its cable with VerletRope.
  * Removed the backdrop ground plane (Plane.008_14).
  * Removed the lid-front decal plane carrying the "Nintendo" wordmark and
    "ENTERTAINMENT SYSTEM" lettering, and cleared the corresponding band of
    NesLabels_baseColor.png. Only functional labels remain (POWER, RESET,
    AUDIO VIDEO, CH3-CH4, RF SWITCH, AC-ADAPTER, 1, 2). The console shell is
    otherwise unchanged geometry by the original author.
  * Renamed shell and control nodes from Sketchfab's "Object_NN" to descriptive
    names so the moving parts can be bound by name.
  * Extracted the controller plug into nes_plug.glb: re-origined to the cable
    end, oriented to insert along -Z, and decimated from 32,118 to 4,069
    triangles.
