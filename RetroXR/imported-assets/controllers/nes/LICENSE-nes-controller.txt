MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted for RetroXR as nes_controller.glb:

  * Scaled to life size (x0.01122); the source measured 12.47 units across
    against a real pad's 0.140 m. The scale is baked into the mesh data and
    local translations, not left on an ancestor node.
  * Flattened each part node's residual Z-up/Y-up rotation into its mesh data so
    every control's local frame is axis-aligned with the root, which is what the
    button-press and D-pad-rocker animation expects.

Geometry, materials and textures are otherwise unchanged. Node and mesh names
are unchanged from the original export.

Nothing here is affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo. The
model's texture carries only functional lettering (A, B, SELECT, START); no
wordmark or logo is present.
