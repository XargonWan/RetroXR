MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted for RetroXR as atari_2600_cart.glb — the cartridge only, taken from the
loose carts in the source scene.

  * ALL LABEL ARTWORK REMOVED. Every cartridge in the source wears a real game's
    printed label, which is third-party copyrighted art and is not redistributable.
    The spine label mesh was deleted outright. The front label mesh was kept as
    geometry only, stripped of its texture, renamed to "media_label" and given a
    blank material — RetroXR paints scraped art onto that face at runtime, the
    same arrangement the NES cart uses. This GLB embeds no images at all.

  * Reoriented to RetroXR's cartridge contract: connector at -Y, body running +Y,
    label facing +Z. The source models the cart lying flat.

  * Shell meshes joined into a single "Cartridge_Body" and re-centred on the
    cart's own middle.

  * Decimated for standalone VR (2026-08-05): 190,822 triangles to 3,995, by the
    same route as the console (see LICENSE-atari-2600-console.txt). "media_label"
    keeps its name, its UVs and its place, so the runtime art still lands on it.
