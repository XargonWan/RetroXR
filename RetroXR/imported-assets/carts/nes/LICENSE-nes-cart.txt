MODIFICATIONS (CC-BY-4.0 requires these be indicated)

Adapted for RetroXR as nes_cart.glb:

  * REMOVED the Super Mario Bros 3 label artwork in full. The source ships the
    whole sticker as one texture (R__0_baseColor.png) — Mario artwork, the
    Nintendo wordmark, the "Official Nintendo Seal of Quality" and the game
    title. That texture, its material, and the two meshes that also carried
    printed branding ("Text", "nintendo") are gone. The exported GLB contains no
    images, textures or samplers at all.
  * The sticker quad itself is kept, renamed "media_label" and given a blank
    matte material, so it serves as the swappable label face RetroXR lays
    scraped box art onto (see cartridge.gd) and reads as an unprinted sticker
    when a game has no art.
  * Re-oriented into ControllerPlug's sibling contract for cartridges: connector
    on -Y, grip +Y, label +Z.
  * Decimated the five bolts from 6,755 triangles each; the model went from
    46,619 triangles to 4,866 overall.
