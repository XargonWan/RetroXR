lamp/chain_pull.wav
    Cut from "Light Switch Chain.aif" by ftpalad — Freesound
    https://freesound.org/people/ftpalad/sounds/119924/
    CC0 1.0 (public domain). Original is 3.90 s, 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo — though
    the two channels are identical (L/R correlation 1.000), so it is flattened to
    mono with no loss.

    The 0.44-1.05 s region: the switch snapping over centre plus the chain
    recoiling afterwards. ONE clip serves both directions, because a pull-chain
    switch is the same detent going over centre either way and genuinely sounds
    the same on as off.

    Normalised x5.1 to peak 0.600 — the source peaks at only 0.118. That lifts
    the noise floor with it, from -62 dBFS to roughly 44 dB below the clip's own
    peak, which is inaudible at the level this plays at. A 3 ms fade at each end
    stops the cut boundary clicking.

    Rejected from the same take: the 2.36-2.98 s region, cut as a separate,
    softer "off". It needed x12.9 to match level, which would have lifted its
    hiss 8 dB more than the on's, and the mechanism does not actually sound
    different in the two directions anyway.

crt/crt_idle.wav, crt/crt_power_on.wav, crt/crt_power_off.wav
    Synthesised by Tools/gen_crt_hum.py from the physics of a CRT set: flyback
    whine at the 15734.264 Hz NTSC line rate, transformer buzz at twice mains,
    broadband hiss, and a degauss thunk at switch-on. Not sampled — every "CRT
    hum" recording found was unlicensed, personal-use-only (BBC RemArc), or
    behind a login with per-file terms.

    The idle loop is exactly periodic by construction, so it wraps seamlessly
    with no crossfade. The transients are built from the same recipe as the loop
    they hand off to, so the tail of the power-on matches the loop's RMS to
    within 0.2%.


=== Import settings are load-bearing ============================================

Every .wav here MUST import with compress/mode=0 (uncompressed PCM). The
importer defaults to QOA, and SpatialAudioEmitter is PCM-push only — PcmClip
decodes to raw frames and refuses anything compressed, so a QOA clip loads as
ZERO frames and plays silence. .gitignore carries an exception so these .import
files ship.
